How it all started

When I was a child, my mum used to organise treasure hunts for my brother and me. We had so much fun that the feeling stayed with me, and as an adult I wanted to bring the same joy to the people I care about. Today I organise a treasure hunt with my friends every year, and it has become a tradition.

I created the first games on this site for evenings with my partner, challenging each other match after match. Then friends and family joined in: for about ten years I have been hosting game nights at home, acting as the "game master" who connects the laptop to the TV, forms the teams and keeps the score. At some point I thought: why not give everyone the chance to do the same?

How the games are made and tested

I start from my own interests, which is why you will find plenty of quizzes on food, films, music and geography. A new game often begins with a simple idea, maybe something I saw on social media, that I turn into a challenge to play together on a TV or a projector.

Every game goes through three stages before it is published:

  1. Development: I write the rules, the questions and the scoring system.
  2. Head-to-head testing: I play it one against one with my partner, and together we work out what to improve, from how points are awarded to questions that are too easy, too hard, too vague or simply wrong, as well as any bugs.
  3. Real-world test: I run it as game master at a real evening with friends, to see how it works with a group and fix the last details.

My favourite

The game I love most is Guess the Top 10: there are lots of possible answers, but you have to be clever enough to work out which one will score the most points. It is very competitive, which is exactly what I enjoy. It is no coincidence that I love competitive team sports like football, volleyball and rugby, and that my favourite board game is Dixit. I live in Emilia-Romagna, in northern Italy.

Content for everyone

Questions and forfeits are written for a general audience: funny, but never offensive or dangerous. All the artwork on the site is made with icons, shapes and emoji generated in code, with no third-party images.
