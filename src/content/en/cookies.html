What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. Similar technologies, such as the browser's localStorage, store information in the same way. On this page we call them all “cookies”.

They are either technical (needed for the site to work, no consent required) or non-technical (third-party statistics and advertising, set only with your consent).

How to manage your consent

On your first visit a banner lets you accept all cookies, reject the non-essential ones or choose each purpose. Closing the banner with the “X” counts as a rejection. You can change your mind at any time:

Your choice is remembered for 180 days. The Site uses Google Consent Mode v2: until you make a choice, no advertising or statistics cookies are activated.
