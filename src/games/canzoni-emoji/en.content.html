How to play Emoji Song Quiz

Emoji Song Quiz is the music quiz where you don't listen to the songs, you decode them. A string of emoji appears on screen, hiding the title of a famous song. For example 👏👏🪨 is We Will Rock You and 🐢🐢🎶 is Despacito (“slowly”). Teams puzzle it out, hum a few bars to check and shout the answer. Guess both the title and the artist to score 2 points. It's a perfect game for parties and dinners with friends of every age, because it mixes classic rock, pop anthems and modern chart-toppers.

Step-by-step rules

  1. Create your teams in the “Your teams” panel and pick the songs: modern hits, classics or all of them.
  2. The emoji puzzle appears. If you want a time limit, press “Start timer”: 30 seconds to answer.
  3. Teams answer out loud: the first complete answer counts.
  4. The host awards points: +2 if the team gets both title and artist, +1 if they only get one.
  5. If nobody gets it, the “Hint” shows the release year. “Show the answer” reveals the title and artist.
  6. “Next song” moves on to the next puzzle. At the end of the night, press “End game” for the leaderboard.

70 songs in emoji

The classics range from Queen, the Beatles and ABBA to Michael Jackson, Madonna, Prince, Nirvana and Oasis. The modern hits feature Adele, Beyoncé, Rihanna, Coldplay, Taylor Swift, Billie Eilish, The Weeknd, Dua Lipa, Harry Styles and many more.

Tips for cracking the puzzles

  • Read the emoji out loud: the title is often a phrase you can translate word by word.
  • Look for colours and numbers: 💜, 💛 or 1️⃣ are very direct clues.
  • Hum it: once you have an idea, try the chorus to see if it fits the emoji.
  • Use hints wisely: you can agree that a song is only worth 1 point after the hint.

Frequently asked questions

Do you hear the songs?

No: the game uses emoji only, with no audio files. If you like, sing the chorus together after the answer is revealed!

How many players do you need?

4 or more people, split into at least two teams. The game supports up to 6 teams.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes: there are songs from every era, so parents and kids can each play to their strengths.

How are points awarded?

With the +2 and +1 buttons next to each team. Each team can only score once per song.
